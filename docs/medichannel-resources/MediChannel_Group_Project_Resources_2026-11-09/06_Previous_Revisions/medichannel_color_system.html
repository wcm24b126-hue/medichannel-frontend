MediChannel · UI/UX design tokens · v1.0

Full Color System

80 tested-ready color tokens across 8 families, including the traditional primary colors (red, yellow and blue). Built for a healthcare booking interface with readable hierarchy, meaningful status cues, and WCAG contrast testing.

Evidence-based design principle: Color associations are context-dependent, not universal psychological laws. Blue is a reasonable healthcare brand starting point, but trust, safety and usability must be validated with actual patients and staff. Never communicate critical status by color alone.

05 · Real-world validation protocol

Before release: run automated contrast checks on every foreground/background pairing; test at 320px mobile width, 200% zoom, light and dark conditions, and color-vision-deficiency simulations. Then observe at least 5 representative patients and staff members completing booking, cancellation, queue tracking and chat tasks; record errors, completion time, comprehension and subjective trust, and revise the palette based on findings.

This palette is a design proposal, not evidence of completed human-subject testing. Do not infer psychological effects from hue alone; test wording, spacing, icons, and status labels together.
